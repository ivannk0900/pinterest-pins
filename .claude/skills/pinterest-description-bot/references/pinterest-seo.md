# Pinterest SEO Best Practices

## Character Limits (Critical)
- **Pin Title:** 100 characters max. First 40 characters show in feed — front-load the most important keyword here.
- **Pin Description:** 500 characters max. First 50-60 characters are the most visible before "more" is clicked — make them count.
- **Board Title:** 50 characters max — use keywords, not cute names.
- **Board Description:** 500 characters max — keyword-rich, describes what content lives there.

## How Pinterest Search Actually Works
Pinterest is a **visual search engine**, not a social media platform. It behaves more like Google than Instagram.

- Pinterest reads text signals: title, description, board name, board description, alt text, and the URL/page content being pinned.
- Keywords in ALL of these locations compound — a pin with keywords in the title + description + board title ranks significantly higher.
- Pinterest rewards **consistent pinning** (3-10 pins/day is optimal for growth) and **fresh content** (new pins outperform repins in search).
- Pinterest SEO takes 30-90 days to compound — it's a long game but results are evergreen.

## Keyword Placement Strategy
**In the title:** Lead with the primary keyword. Example: "Content Marketing Plan for Small Business Owners" not "A Plan for Your Content Marketing."

**In the description:**
- Use primary keyword in the first sentence
- Use secondary keywords naturally throughout
- Use long-tail keywords in the middle/end sections
- Never keyword stuff — aim for natural readability with 2-5 keyword phrases per description

**Keyword types to include per pin:**
1. Short-tail (1-2 words): e.g. "content marketing"
2. Long-tail (3-5 words): e.g. "content marketing plan for beginners"
3. Question-based (if relevant): e.g. "how to create a content marketing strategy"

## Algorithm Signals Pinterest Rewards
- **Saves (repins):** The #1 signal of quality content. Write descriptions that make people want to save.
- **Click-throughs:** Pinterest rewards pins that drive traffic off-platform. CTAs matter.
- **Engagement rate:** Saves + clicks + comments all compound.
- **Consistency:** Accounts that pin regularly outperform sporadic pinners.
- **Fresh pins:** New pin images pointing to existing URLs still count as "fresh content."

## What Pinterest Penalises
- ❌ Hashtags (Pinterest has confirmed these hurt reach — NEVER use them)
- ❌ Spammy keyword stuffing (unnatural repetition tanks distribution)
- ❌ Duplicate descriptions (vary descriptions across pins pointing to same URL)
- ❌ Broken links (Pinterest actively suppresses pins leading to 404 errors)
- ❌ Low-quality outbound pages (thin content pages get lower distribution)

## Seasonal & Trending Content
Pinterest users search **45-60 days ahead** of key dates/seasons. A Christmas pin needs to go live in late October. A back-to-school pin in late June. Timing content ahead of trends is a major advantage on Pinterest vs other platforms.

## Idea Pin vs Standard Pin
- **Standard pins** (link pins) = best for driving traffic to a URL. This is what these descriptions are written for.
- **Idea pins** (no external link) = better for follower growth and engagement but don't drive traffic. Not the focus of this skill.
