# Power Words & Emotional Triggers Bank

## How to Use This File
Every pin title and description should include at least 1-2 power words. These words trigger an emotional or psychological response that makes people stop scrolling, click, or save. Choose words that match the emotional tone of the content and the audience.

---

## CURIOSITY TRIGGERS
Make people feel like they're about to learn something they don't know yet.

secretly | little-known | surprising | unexpected | hidden | counterintuitive | rarely discussed | most people don't know | what nobody tells you | the truth about | what experts won't say | behind the scenes | what actually works | the real reason | you've been doing it wrong

## FEAR OF MISSING OUT (FOMO)

---

## SPECIFICITY TRIGGERS
Numbers and specifics build credibility and curiosity.

Use specific numbers wherever possible:
- "7 ways" beats "some ways"
- "Increase traffic by 43%" beats "increase traffic"
- "In 30 days" beats "quickly"
- "Under $50" beats "affordable"
- "5-minute" beats "fast"

Power number phrases: in just X days | X simple steps | X mistakes | X secrets | X tools | X templates | X reasons why | by X% | under X minutes
